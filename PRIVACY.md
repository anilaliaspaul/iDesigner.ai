# Privacy Policy for iDesigner.ai

**Effective date:** November 15, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0.0

## 1. Overview

iDesigner.ai is a brand-aware creative design plugin intended to run with supported AI coding assistants, including Claude Code and OpenAI Codex. It provides design workflows, brand-guideline resources, templates, and related local project assets.

This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the plugin. The plugin is distributed as software and does not, by itself, operate a separate iDesigner.ai cloud account, hosted design service, or central user database.

## 2. Information you provide

Depending on the task you ask it to perform, the AI assistant using iDesigner.ai may access or process information you provide or make available in your workspace, such as:

- Design briefs, prompts, instructions, and approved copy.
- Brand guidelines, logos, photographs, fonts, and other design assets.
- Project files and content you ask the assistant to read, create, or modify.
- Output files and design-related metadata created during your workflow.

Treat confidential, personal, or otherwise sensitive material carefully. Only provide information and assets that you are authorised to use.

## 3. AI assistant and provider processing

The AI assistant that runs iDesigner.ai may send prompts, relevant workspace content, and other information needed to fulfil your request to its service provider. For example, use with Claude Code or OpenAI Codex is subject to the applicable provider's terms, privacy policy, account settings, and data-handling practices.

The provider may process information to generate responses or perform requested tasks. iDesigner.ai does not control the provider's retention, security, or training practices. Review the applicable provider documentation before using the plugin with confidential or sensitive information.

## 4. Third-party integrations

iDesigner.ai may include optional integrations with third-party services. The distributed configuration includes a Canva Model Context Protocol (MCP) endpoint. If you enable or use the Canva integration, information necessary for the action you request may be exchanged with Canva. Canva's own terms and privacy policy govern its handling of that information.

Third-party integrations are optional and may require separate authentication or permissions. Do not connect an account or grant access unless you understand and approve the requested permissions.

## 5. Local files and workspace access

The plugin's workflows may instruct the AI assistant to inspect, create, or update files in the workspace you are using. The assistant should perform file operations relevant to your request and subject to the permissions and safeguards of the host application and your environment.

The plugin package does not independently upload your local files to an iDesigner.ai-operated server. However, the AI assistant or a third-party integration may transmit relevant content to its provider when required to perform a task, as described above.

## 6. Collection, analytics, and storage by iDesigner.ai

The iDesigner.ai plugin package does not intentionally collect telemetry, run advertising trackers, or maintain its own user profile database or hosted storage service. It does not provide a separate account-registration system.

This statement concerns the plugin package itself. It does not describe data collected by Claude, OpenAI, Canva, GitHub, or other services you choose to use. Those services operate under their own policies.

## 7. Sharing

The plugin's publisher does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the AI assistant provider or by integrations you choose to use, as necessary to carry out your instructions. You are responsible for reviewing the permissions and policies of those services.

## 8. Security

No software or online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your AI assistant, connected accounts, devices, and development environment secure. Do not place API keys, passwords, access tokens, or other secrets in prompts, public repositories, or design assets. Use the host application's supported secret-management features where available.

## 9. Children's privacy

iDesigner.ai is a general-purpose design tool and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. If you use it to create materials involving children, avoid including unnecessary personal details and ensure you have the appropriate authority and consent for any photographs or information you use.

## 10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the plugin's features, integrations, or data practices change. The current version will be published in the iDesigner.ai GitHub repository. The effective date and release version at the top of this document identify the current revision.
