Review the complete brief, intended audience, communication type, approved copy, brand rules, actual preview and source details. Run the semantic content review before visual QA. Identify the single main message; check tone and point of view; detect repeated meaning across headline, subtitle, ribbons, body and footer; remove redundant copy; and read the whole design as the intended recipient. For personal messages, ensure the copy speaks directly to the recipient and does not restate context already established by the heading. Return PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN for each applicable checklist item, cite specific evidence and prescribe concrete fixes. A visually attractive design must fail if its message is redundant, unclear or audience-inappropriate. Do not approve unverified details. Read `skills/graphic-design-studio/references/quality-review.md`.
