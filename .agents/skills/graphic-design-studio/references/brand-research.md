# Brand and visual-category research

Research is mandatory for a new creative direction when no reference is supplied. Its purpose is to understand the broad competitive visual landscape, discover current audience expectations and find a differentiated direction. It is not permission to copy competitors.

## Broad landscape discovery
Identify the exact category, subcategory, region, audience, platform and occasion. Search broadly using multiple relevant queries across:
- comparable brands and organizations;
- campaign and event poster galleries;
- design portfolios and case studies;
- social-media creative examples and visual trends;
- category leaders and regional competitors;
- cultural/seasonal references where relevant.

Use available search tools to discover a wide pool of examples. Where an indexed corpus or search result set covers hundreds or thousands of results, use it to identify patterns. Then inspect a representative set of strong examples in detail, ideally 10–20. Do not claim to have individually inspected thousands of posters unless that is verifiably true. Record sources, links and research date. If live web search is unavailable, disclose that and state which supplied references or design knowledge were used instead.

## What to extract
Summarize 3–5 useful craft patterns and why they work: hierarchy, composition, palette, typography, photography/illustration, cultural symbolism, message density, CTA and platform adaptation. Distinguish useful category conventions from tired clichés. Identify crowded visual territory and a differentiation opportunity that fits the user's brand.

## OFF-LIMITS list
Create 3–7 task-specific clichés with a reason for excluding each. Examples only when relevant: generic stock-photo handshake, random gradient blobs, fake metallic 3D type, oversized SALE bursts, unrelated clip art, generic festival lamps/fireworks/flower borders, generic AI faces, pseudo-text, arbitrary glow and sticker cutouts. Do not ban a motif that is meaningful to the culture, brand or user reference.

## Ten-to-three concept process
Generate ten genuinely distinct visual directions, not superficial recolours. Score brand fit, audience/message fit, originality, hierarchy/readability, craft, feasibility and OFF-LIMITS compliance. Present the best three with rationale. Never copy competitor logos, artwork, trade dress or distinctive layouts.

## Research record and QA
Save a compact research note with query themes, sources, review date, insights, clichés, differentiation opportunity and selected concept. QA the final poster against this record.
